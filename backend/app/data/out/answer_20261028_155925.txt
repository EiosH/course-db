答案:
The **D2L notification system** is the best example.

On the slide, **temporally coupled + referentially decoupled** corresponds to an **event-based** system: components must be active at the same time, but they don’t need to know each other’s identities. Linda tuple spaces are shared data space, email is mailbox-style, and instant messaging is direct.
