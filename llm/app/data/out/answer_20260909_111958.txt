答案:
Honestly, the lecture doesn't really offer a simpler, purely functional example to replace this specific code. The instructor uses this exact implementation of `foldLeft` and `foldRight` in Scala as the *primary* example to illustrate why it's considered "glorified iteration" (or "lipstick on a pig") because it relies on `var` and `while` loops under the hood.

The main point being made is that in this context, folds aren't giving you the pure functional programming benefits you might expect (like early abortion of processing), so there isn't a "cleaner" version shown here that fixes that—it's just showing you the reality of the implementation.
