They broke it down into two sub-expressions:
*   **E1**: The first number (5), which evaluates to **V1** (5).
*   **E2**: The second number (3), which evaluates to **V2** (3).

The instructor explained that E1 is evaluated first, potentially updating the variable store (from $\xi_0$ to $\xi_1$), and then E2 is evaluated (from $\xi_1$ to $\xi_2$) to get its value.
